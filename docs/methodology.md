
# Research Methodology

## Study Design

This project uses a population-based pharmacogenomic research approach to investigate CYP2C19*2 (rs4244285) and its relationship with clopidogrel response.

## Study Components

### 1. Variant Investigation
- Gene: CYP2C19
- Variant: CYP2C19*2
- Reference SNP ID: rs4244285
- Variant description: c.681G>A
- Functional classification: No-function allele

### 2. Population Analysis

Three population datasets were examined:

- gnomAD: Broad genetic ancestry groups.
- 1000 Genomes: Selected South Asian populations.
- HGDP: Selected population groups.

### 3. Allele Frequency

Allele frequency was calculated or represented using:

AF (%) = (Allele Count / Allele Number) × 100

### 4. Clinical Interpretation

The clinical implications of CYP2C19 variation were examined using the CPIC guideline for CYP2C19 genotype and clopidogrel therapy.

## Limitations

- Population datasets have different sampling designs.
- Sample sizes vary across population groups.
- Allele frequency does not represent carrier prevalence.
- Population-level findings cannot independently predict individual clinical response.
